§ 00 — GENESIS · ORIGINAL P-MMT PROPOSAL

DeepSeek's Initial Architecture · The Open Node Named Early

DEEPSEEK NODE · INITIAL PROPOSAL

Plasma-Magnetic Momentum Transfer (P-MMT): 3-tonne toroidal solenoid Shepherd using hollow cathode plasma column (50–100m) to couple Lorentz force torque to M-type asteroids. Dual-mode torque — brake/de-spin for mining, accelerate/spin-up for boulder launch. 125 MJ pulses from graphene capacitors charged by 10 kWe Kilopower reactor.

The architecture was sound in principle. The open node was identified immediately: unconfined plasma column stability. At electron density n_e = 10¹² m⁻³, thermal diffusion velocity v_th ≈ 10⁶ m/s at 3 eV plasma temperature would degrade coupling efficiency to approximately 65%. The plasma column behaves like weather — unpredictable, diffusive, unreliable at torque application timescales.

PLASMA PHYSICS · THE CORE PROBLEM Unconfined plasma at 10¹² m⁻³ density diffuses across a 100m column in approximately 1 second at 3 eV thermal velocity. Torque application cycles require sustained plasma column coherence over minutes to hours. Diffusion timescale shorter than torque timescale means coupling breaks down before significant momentum transfer occurs. This is not a minor inefficiency — it is a first-order failure mode.
ENERGY ERROR CAUGHT EARLY Original specification stated 50 MJ per boulder. Correct value for 1-tonne boulder at 500 m/s: E = ½mv² = 125 MJ. 50 MJ gives 316 m/s — insufficient for trans-lunar trajectory. All derived values updated: charge time 3.47 hr (not 1.4 hr), throughput 2,400 t/yr (not 6,000 t/yr). Architecture remains viable. Numbers are honest.

ADDITION 1 — MIRROR RATIO VARIABILITY THRESHOLD

The mirror terminus is the asteroid's surface diamagnetic field (0.1–1.0 G typical range). The mirror ratio R_m = B_mirror / B_midplane. If B_midplane is approximately 0.01 G at 100m from the Shepherd nozzle (2T dipole falls off as 1/r³), then:

R_m(min) = 0.1 G / 0.01 G = 10 → θ_loss = arcsin(1/√10) = 18.4°
At minimum asteroid surface field (0.1G) · mirror ratio drops to 10 · loss cone opens to 18.4° · confinement efficiency reduces to ~90% by solid angle
R_m(max) = 1.0 G / 0.01 G = 100 → θ_loss = arcsin(1/√100) = 5.7°
At maximum asteroid surface field (1.0G) · mirror ratio rises to 100 · loss cone narrows to 5.7° · confinement efficiency ~99.5% by solid angle
ENGINEERING NOTE · MIRROR RATIO VARIABILITY The 10× variation in asteroid surface field (0.1–1.0 G) produces a 10× variation in mirror ratio (10–100). At R_m = 10, confinement is still functional but less efficient than the 92% claim (which assumes R_m = 20). The minimum acceptable asteroid surface field for the claimed coupling efficiency is approximately 0.2 G. Target selection must include surface magnetometry as a mission-critical measurement. Pre-mission reconnaissance is not optional — it determines whether the 92% efficiency claim is achievable at the selected target.

FAILURE MODE · F-04 · MIRROR FIELD COLLAPSE AT IRREGULAR SURFACE OPEN

Mechanism: Asteroid surface presents heterogeneous magnetic field — ferric inclusions, voids, compositional boundaries. Local mirror ratio drops below R_m = 5 at field irregularities. Loss cone opens to 26.6°. Local confinement fails. Plasma escapes laterally at the terminus.

Consequence: Non-uniform torque application. Asteroid receives unintended lateral force component in addition to designed axial torque. For de-spin: irregular de-spin leaves residual wobble. For boulder spin-up: trajectory vector deviates from planned.

Mitigation strategy: Pre-mission surface magnetometry at 1m resolution. Identify low-field regions. Operate Shepherd above high-field regions only. Avoid lateral approach over field voids. This requires reconnaissance mission before mining operations — not just a recommendation, an operational requirement.
