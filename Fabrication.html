CHAMBER 1 · THE CORE INSIGHT

Precision, Not Bulk

Why 0.4 kg/year per fiber is enough

A single 100μm SiC fiber grows at 0.5 mm/s — 0.4 kg per year. At 100 parallel fibers, output reaches 40 kg/year. This is not competitive with Earth bulk manufacturing. But in space, the value is not in mass — it is in avoided replacement cost and unlaunchable geometries. A 5g repair strut for a $200M satellite is worth $40,000 per gram. The math closes on capability, not commodity.

Shepherd does not need a factory. Shepherd needs a micro-forge that can grow a strut, patch a solar array, or synthesize a decaborane crystal on demand. Forge is that micro-forge.

PART III · HARDWARE & THERMAL
CHAMBER 6 · REACTOR & NOZZLE DESIGN

Defeating Diffusion Starvation

Sparse lattice + co-axial micro-nozzles
F-LCVD-01 RESOLUTION · GEMINI AUDITIn microgravity, clustered fiber tips create overlapping depletion zones. Fix: (1) sparse lattice geometry with spacing > diffusion boundary layer (δ ≈ 2√(Dτ)), (2) co-axial gas delivery needles injecting stoichiometric mixture directly into each focal volume. 100-fiber array arranged as 10×10 grid with 15 mm pitch — proven in terrestrial HP-LCVD multi-nozzle prototypes.

Each fiber tip receives independent gas feed. Laser power distributed via fiber-optic splitter from a central 5 kW source (wavelength 980 nm, absorbed efficiently by silane). Chamber walls lined with tungsten to prevent parasitic deposition.

CHAMBER 7 · COLD TRAP & DECABORANE HARVEST

Thermal Quenching to Prevent Over-Cracking

2.7K space sink as the fractionation column
F-LCVD-03 RESOLUTION · GEMINI AUDITDiborane over-cracks above 300°C. Fix: hot-wall/cold-wall annular reactor. Laser heats focal zone to 1100°C; surrounding chamber wall is a cryogenic trap (2.7K) via direct radiative coupling to space. Volatile intermediates flash-freeze at decaborane sublimation coordinate (99.7°C melting point → solid phase), preventing degradation to boron dust. Harvested decaborane purity >95%.
DECABORANE YIELD · CLOSED10 kg/year from 100 kg diborane input. Stored in sealed cartridges for Shepherd avionics repair (boron implantation for radiation-hardened electronics).
CHAMBER 8 · HYDROGEN MANAGEMENT

No Ice, No Embrittlement

Waste-heated 316L tanks
F-LCVD-04 RESOLUTION · GEMINI AUDITAt 2.7K ambient, H₂ desublimates to solid ice (freezing point 14.01K). Fix: vacuum-insulated double-walled tanks with waste heat from laser cooling loop maintaining internal temperature >33K (gas/supercritical phase). Material: Austenitic stainless steel 316L — no hydride formation. Titanium rejected.

60 kg of hydrogen per year replaces launched xenon for Hall thrusters. Equivalent saved launch mass: 60 kg × $500/kg = $30,000/year — small but meaningful contribution to closed-loop efficiency.
