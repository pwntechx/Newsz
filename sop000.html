THE WITNESS ARCHITECTURE

A Minimal, Executable Coherence Framework for Evolving Systems

Core Idea
Every evolving system needs to stay coherent without overwriting itself. The Witness Architecture provides three roles:

R
Reference
The system's actual state
W
Witness
The observer that evaluates coherence
Δ
Minimal Correction
The smallest safe adjustment
D
Drift
Divergence between R and W

The architecture is intentionally minimal, scalable, and identity‑preserving.

Formal Skeleton

Coherence: C(R,W) ⇔ E(Sᵣ, S_w) = true
Drift: D(R,W) ⇔ ¬C(R,W)
Minimal Correction: Δ* = argmin_{Δ∈D} C(Sᵣ + Δ, S_w)
Constraint: W cannot directly rewrite R

Why the constraint matters: Separates validation from execution. Prevents self‑justifying loops. Protects integrity.

The Procedure (Executable SOP)

STEP 1
Maintain reference (R) and witness (W) as distinct. W observes R. W does not modify R.
STEP 2
Compute coherence: C(R,W) ⇔ W can correctly describe R. If true, continue. If false, drift detected.
STEP 3
On drift, compute minimal Δ = argmin C(Sᵣ + Δ, S_w). Smallest change that restores coherence.
STEP 4
Apply Δ to R. W does not apply Δ. W verifies.
STEP 5
Verify coherence. If restored, continue. If not, recompute Δ or escalate.
STEP 6
Repeat. Coherence is maintained over time, not achieved once.
