§ 02b · HISTORICAL TEXT REFERENCE AS GEOMETRY

Historical continuity (Perplexity addition): One reason this field is hard to dismiss is that the same patterns appear across historical periods. Before the modern UFO vocabulary, there were reports of angels, demons, airships, strange visitors, and luminous presences. The language changes. The pattern often does not.

Selected pre-20th century references:

  • Livy (218 BCE): "phantom ships in the sky" over Rome
  • Nuremberg 1561: Spherical, cylindrical, and cross-like objects in aerial battle
  • Basle 1566: "Black spheres" moving rapidly before sunrise
  • Airship waves (1896–1897, USA): Thousands of reports of unknown dirigibles

That does not prove every old story points to the same cause. It does suggest human testimony may be repeatedly interfacing with something that is culturally translated into available forms. In one era, the wardrobe is religious. In another, it is technological. In another, it is biological. But the encounter shape remains recognizable.
